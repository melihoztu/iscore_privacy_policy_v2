Delete Your Account

iScore Football · Melih Öztürk

iScore can be used entirely without an account. Live scores, fixtures, tables and statistics require no sign-in. If you did create an account — through Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google — this page explains how to delete it and what happens to your data.

1. Delete directly in the app Fastest

If you still have iScore installed, deletion takes a few seconds and needs no request or waiting time:

  1. Open iScore and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account card, tap Account settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm in the dialog that appears. If you signed in with Apple, the Apple dialog appears once more — this is what allows us to revoke the Sign in with Apple grant as well.

Your account and the associated data are deleted immediately. The app continues to work afterwards, simply without an account.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you have already uninstalled iScore or cannot access your account, send us an email from the address you signed in with:

Email: iscoresport@gmail.com

Subject: Account deletion request

Please send the request from the email address linked to your account so we can identify it. We do not need any other personal details.

We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. If we cannot match your email address to an account, we will tell you instead of deleting anything.

3. What is deleted

The following is removed permanently and cannot be restored:

Data Where it was stored
Email address, display name, account ID Supabase (EU)
Sign in with Apple / Google grant Revoked with Apple / Google
Smart Search questions and usage counters Our servers
Subscription link to your account RevenueCat

Favourite teams, interface settings and other preferences are stored only on your device and never reach our servers. They disappear when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. What we have to keep Legally required

Deleting your account does not remove records we are legally obliged to retain:

Data Retention period Reason
Billing and transaction records for iScore Plus 10 years Commercial and tax retention obligations (§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB)

These records are kept in restricted storage: they are no longer used for any purpose other than meeting the retention obligation, and they are deleted once the period expires.

Your subscription itself is managed by Apple or Google, not by us. Deleting your iScore account does not cancel a running subscription. Please cancel it in your Apple Account settings or in the Google Play Store, otherwise it continues to renew.

5. Anonymous data

Advertising and measurement data collected by Google AdMob in the free version is not linked to your iScore account and cannot be assigned to you by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings, and manage your consent choice inside the app under Settings.
